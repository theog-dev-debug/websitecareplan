How we work

The same terms on every plan

You own everything

Your .id domain, hosting, Google profile, analytics, code and content are registered to your business, and the agreement is in Bahasa Indonesia and English. We work through access you grant, and you can hand it to another provider at any time with our handover pack and site runbook.

One place for requests

Send every request through one agreed channel, such as a WhatsApp group or email, and each one is logged. Response times follow your plan, and outages are handled under its incident response.

Edits versus new work

Included hours cover changes to existing pages: text, images, hours, prices and notices. New pages, features and redesigns are quoted and approved before work starts.

No surprise bills

Prices are in rupiah. Domains, hosting, licences and other services are either paid by you directly or billed through us, each listed with its cost and renewal date. Tax treatment, including PPh 23, is stated in your proposal.
